# Agentic Cinema: The Blockbuster Hackathon

**Source:** [Official Devpost Hackathon Page](https://agentic-cinema.devpost.com/)

## 1. Hackathon Overview & Objective
- **Goal:** Build a functional, production-ready AI agent or multi-agent system that solves a real-world bottleneck in a media and entertainment workflow.
- **Scope:** Examples of target workflows include script processing, video transcription, storyboarding, asset management, production data management, and studio crew assistance. 
- **Constraint:** This is a competition to build *functional tools* for media and entertainment, *not* a competition to submit AI-generated films.

## 2. Technology Stack & Requirements
To qualify, your project must strictly adhere to these technology requirements:
- **Core AI Platform:** The solution must be powered by **Google Cloud** and the **Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform**.
- **Partner Integration:** You must integrate one of the five official partner technologies: **IBM, Grafana Labs, Parallel, ClickHouse, or Replit**. The project must demonstrate actual runtime use of your chosen partner's product or Model Context Protocol (MCP) server.
- **Allowed Tools:** You may only use Google Cloud AI tools and the built-in AI features of your chosen partner. For AI-assisted coding, only the **Gemini CLI** and **Gemini Code Assist** are permitted.
- **Prohibited Tools:** You may **not** use other external AI models, third-party agent frameworks, or other external AI APIs (including those used for writing code).

## 3. Submission Materials
A complete and valid submission on Devpost must include all of the following:
- **Hosted Project:** A public URL pointing to a live, hosted, functional version of your project.
- **Demo Video:** A roughly 3-minute video demonstrating the solution and its workflow in action.
- **Code Repository:** A public code repository (e.g., GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket) with a complete open-source license. The codebase must clearly demonstrate the runtime use of both Google Cloud and the chosen partner's service.
- **Partner Track Selection:** Clear specification of which of the five partner tracks your project falls under.
- **Submission Form:** A fully completed Devpost submission form.

## 4. Judging Criteria
Projects are evaluated based on the following pillars:
- **Technological Implementation:** Assessment of multi-step autonomous AI workflows, multi-agent collaboration, and production-ready architecture.
- **Design & User Experience:** The cohesiveness, usability, interface, and user-centric approach of the final product.
- **Potential Impact:** The practical value, scale, and long-term viability of the tool for enterprise-level media industry workflows.
- **Creativity & Innovation:** The originality of the approach in solving complex real-world problems autonomously, moving beyond simple chatbots.

## 5. Eligibility & Deadlines
- **Deadline:** Submissions must be finalized by **September 9, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. PDT** (Note: Verification on the Devpost dashboard is recommended, as some early announcements cited Sept 7).
- **Eligibility:** Participants must be of legal age of majority in their country of residence and located in a Devpost-supported country or territory.
- **Original Work:** All code and submitted projects must be original work.

## 6. Prizes
- **Total Prize Pool:** $75,000, distributed evenly across the five partner tracks.
- **Track Prizes:** Within each track, the prizes are $7,500 for 1st place, $4,500 for 2nd place, and $3,000 for 3rd place.
